RADIO MEDICAL COMMUNICATION PROCEDURES

Before contact:
- Prepare patient identity, scenario summary, and timeline.
- Gather latest ABCDE findings and key vitals.
- List treatments already performed and available onboard resources.

During contact:
- Present concise structured report (ABCDE + interventions + trend).
- Communicate red flags first.
- Clarify uncertain details rather than guessing.

After guidance:
- Read back critical instructions to confirm understanding.
- Record medication doses, timing, and reassessment plan.
- Define next report interval and escalation triggers.

Documentation essentials:
- UTC date/time of each major event.
- Exact values for vitals and changes over time.
- Objective response to interventions (improved/worse/unchanged).

Escalation cues:
- Deteriorating airway or breathing.
- Persistent hypoxia, hypotension, or altered consciousness.
- Suspected internal bleeding, stroke signs, or chest pain with instability.
