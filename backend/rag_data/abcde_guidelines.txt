ABCDE MARITIME MEDICAL ASSESSMENT GUIDELINES

A - Airway
- Check if airway is patent and protected.
- If compromised, apply jaw thrust/chin lift and suction as needed.
- Consider oropharyngeal airway when indicated.

B - Breathing
- Assess breathing rate, pattern, and chest movement.
- Record SpO2 and oxygen delivery method and flow.
- Escalate urgently for severe respiratory distress or hypoxia.

C - Circulation
- Measure pulse, blood pressure, capillary refill, and skin signs.
- Identify shock indicators early (hypotension, tachycardia, poor perfusion).
- Start IV access and fluid strategy per protocol when indicated.

D - Disability
- Assess consciousness level and neurological red flags.
- Record pupil findings and seizure activity.

E - Exposure
- Perform full-body check while preventing hypothermia.
- Document temperature and environmental factors.

RADIO MEDICAL REPORT REMINDERS
- Include objective measurements and timestamps.
- State what interventions were already done and patient response.
- Explicitly call out red flags and escalation needs.
