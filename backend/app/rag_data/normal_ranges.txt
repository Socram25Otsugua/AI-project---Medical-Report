CLINICAL NORMAL RANGES REFERENCE
Maritime Medical Quick Reference Guide

=== VITAL SIGNS NORMAL RANGES ===

Heart Rate (Pulse):
- Normal adult: 60-80 beats per minute
- Bradycardia: below 60 bpm
- Tachycardia: above 100 bpm
- Critical tachycardia: above 130 bpm
- Critical bradycardia: below 40 bpm

Breathing Rate (Respiratory Rate):
- Normal adult: 12-16 breaths per minute
- Slow (bradypnoea): below 12 bpm
- Fast (tachypnoea): above 20 bpm
- Critical: above 30 bpm or below 8 bpm

Oxygen Saturation (SpO2):
- Normal: 95-100%
- Mild hypoxia: 90-94% - oxygen required
- Moderate hypoxia: 85-89% - high flow oxygen, monitor closely
- Severe hypoxia: below 85% - critical, consider MEDEVAC
- COPD patients: 88-92% may be acceptable

Blood Pressure:
- Normal systolic: 120-140 mmHg
- Normal diastolic: 60-90 mmHg
- Hypertension: systolic above 140
- Hypotension: systolic below 90
- Shock: systolic below 80 - emergency
- Hypertensive crisis: systolic above 180

Temperature:
- Normal oral: 36.5 degrees Celsius
- Normal range: 36.0-37.5 degrees Celsius
- Low grade fever: 37.5-38.0
- Fever: above 38.0
- High fever: above 39.0
- Hyperthermia: above 40.0 - emergency cooling
- Mild hypothermia: 32-35 degrees
- Moderate hypothermia: 28-32 degrees - emergency
- Severe hypothermia: below 28 degrees - critical

Capillary Refill Time:
- Normal: less than 2 seconds
- Prolonged: 2-3 seconds - monitor closely
- Poor perfusion: above 3 seconds - concern for shock

=== CONSCIOUSNESS LEVELS ===
Level 1: Awake, alert, well oriented - normal
Level 2: Confused, responds to questions - concerning
Level 3: Responds to pain only - serious
Level 4: Unconscious, no response - critical, MEDEVAC likely

=== COMMON MARITIME MEDICAL SCENARIOS ===

Hypothermia (cold water immersion):
- Remove wet clothing immediately
- Warm gradually - avoid rapid rewarming
- Handle gently - risk of cardiac arrhythmia
- Monitor core temperature continuously
- Level 3-4 hypothermia: MEDEVAC required

Trauma (falls, crush injuries):
- Immobilize spine if mechanism suggests injury
- Control bleeding with direct pressure
- Monitor for internal bleeding signs
- Pain management within medicine chest
- Photograph injuries if possible

Chest Pain:
- 12-lead ECG if available
- Aspirin 300mg if cardiac cause suspected and no contraindications
- Oxygen if SpO2 below 95%
- Rest, semi-recumbent position
- Contact Radio Medical immediately

Diabetic Emergency:
- Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar): glucose if conscious, glucagon if not
- Normal blood sugar: 4-7 mmol/liter
- Below 4: hypoglycemia - sugar/glucose
- Above 15: hyperglycemia - insulin if available, fluids, Radio Medical
