RADIO MEDICAL PROCEDURES
Danish Maritime Authority - Telemedicine Guidelines

=== CONTACTING RADIO MEDICAL ===
Radio Medical Denmark provides 24/7 medical advice to ships at sea.
Contact should be made when:
- Any serious illness or injury occurs on board
- The medical officer is uncertain about treatment
- Patient condition is deteriorating
- MEDEVAC may be required

Required information when calling:
- Ship name, call sign, and position (coordinates)
- Satellite phone number and ship email
- Patient name, age, gender, nationality
- Description of problem: what happened, when, where, symptoms
- Current vital signs (ABCDE assessment)
- Medications given before contact
- Distance and ETA to nearest port

=== REPORTING FORMAT ===
Always report in ABCDE order:
1. Airway: is it clear, what interventions were done, oxygen given
2. Breathing: rate, depth, oxygen saturation
3. Circulation: pulse rate and location, blood pressure, skin color, capillary refill
4. Disability: consciousness level, pupils, convulsions, paralysis
5. Expose: temperature, injuries found, top-to-toe performed

=== MEDEVAC CRITERIA ===
Request MEDEVAC (medical evacuation) when:
- Patient is unconscious or consciousness level 3-4
- Oxygen saturation below 88% despite maximum oxygen
- Systolic blood pressure below 80 mmHg despite fluid resuscitation
- Heart rate above 140 or below 40 despite treatment
- Suspected internal bleeding or major trauma
- Severe chest pain with ECG changes
- Stroke symptoms: facial droop, arm weakness, speech difficulty
- Severe burns covering more than 15% body surface
- Obstetric emergency
- Any condition where delay threatens life

=== FOLLOW-UP REPORTING ===
After initial contact, report back as instructed by Radio Medical.
Typical intervals:
- Critical patients (level 3-4): every 15 minutes
- Serious patients (level 2): every 30 minutes
- Stable patients (level 1): every 60 minutes
- Always report immediately if condition changes

=== DOCUMENTATION ===
Document all observations and treatments on the Radio Medical Record.
Include times for all interventions.
Keep record for medicolegal purposes.
Send completed form by email to Radio Medical after contact.
